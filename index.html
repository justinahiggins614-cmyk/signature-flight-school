📖 How It Works

1 · Sign in

Choose a pilot name above. Your hours and flights are saved on this device.

2 · Learn the controls

Captain Siggy's lessons teach the stick (up/down/left/right), buttons 1–11, and keyboard keys — in plain words.

3 · Fly the sim

Take off, turn, land. Gentle landings earn praise; crashes earn coaching. Every flight logs hours.

4 · Fly solo

Turn off the AI helper and prove you can do it alone. Your logbook shows both.
