Welcome to The Signature Flight School

A real flight simulator — pick any of 12 aircraft, train with lessons, and log real pilot hours. Here’s how to use it:

  1. Pick your aircraft. 12 planes and jets on the Aircraft page — each one flies like itself.
  2. Learn the lessons. Step-by-step training with Captain Siggy, your AI instructor — from first takeoff to landing.
  3. Fly the sim. Stick plus buttons 1–11, throttle and flaps — real flight physics, map view, and jet-game-grade visual effects.
  4. Watch, then fly. AI demo flights show any maneuver first — end your flight to log hours on your device.

How to use this site

✈️ Aircraft

What: the Aircraft page. Does: 12 planes and jets, each with its own flight model and look. How: Open Aircraft, pick one, press Fly.

🎓 Lessons

What: the training track. Does: Step-by-step lessons from takeoff to landing with Captain Siggy the AI instructor. How: Start Lesson 1 and follow the calls.

🕹 Sim controls

What: the cockpit controls. Does: Stick for pitch and roll, buttons 1–11 for views and systems (7 = map, 10 = pause), throttle for power — honest flight physics. How: Fly in the sim; the button bar labels every control.

🧬 AI demo flights

What: the watch-first mode. Does: The AI flies any maneuver or path so you can study it, then you take the controls. How: Press “Watch the AI fly it” in the sim.

🗺 Flight Paths

What: the Paths page. Does: Watch the AI fly any flight path, then fly it yourself. How: Open Flight Paths, pick a path.

⏱️ Pilot hours

What: your logbook. Does: Flight time is logged on your device when you end a flight. How: Press “End flight & log hours”.

🎨 Graphics tiers

What: the visuals dial. Does: 16-BIT up to BEYOND — sky, clouds, afterburner, vapor trails scale with the era you pick. How: The graphics control in the sim; lower tiers also keep older phones smooth.
